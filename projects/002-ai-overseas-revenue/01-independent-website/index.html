SRT Subtitle Checker

Check Chinese, English or bilingual subtitles before you publish or hand them off.

An SRT is a text file that tells a player what to display and when. Because the subtitles are separate from the video, you can edit them or add another language without exporting the video again.

    What this tool checks

    Timing
    Find overlapping cues, reversed times and cues with zero duration.
    File structure
    Find malformed timecodes, missing cue numbers, missing separators and empty cues.
    Readability
    Flag long lines, fast reading speeds and cues that appear too briefly or too long.

    Use the cue number and source line to fix issues in your subtitle editor. Review the video separately to confirm that the words match the audio and picture.
